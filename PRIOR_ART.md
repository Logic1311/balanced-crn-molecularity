# Literature and novelty audit — 2026-11-26 (JST)

## Decision and scope

The focused manuscript investigates the minimum molecularity of ordinary, deterministic, same-species, edge-wise conservative complex-/detailed-balanced realizations **on a prescribed single two-species simplex**. The principal candidate contribution is the exact degree-cap certificate, the explicit parity-dependent cubic threshold, and the resulting unbounded gap despite fixed polynomial degree. This is not a priority certificate. Known global realization, collinear-complex, positivity and directional-sign results are credited. The original broader epidemic investigation is preserved separately and is not represented as solved in full.

## Gate assessment

- Mathematical core: exact finite-matrix equivalence, parity thresholds, and global/restricted counterexample have machine-checked proofs; independent rational and numerical tests support implementation fidelity. The scoped manuscript identifies analytic-only auxiliary statements rather than labeling them Lean-verified. Version 0.4 additionally checks low-cap infeasibility, finite DB existence for all interior equilibrium locations, and global/fixed separation at an arbitrary prescribed interior balancing point.
- Originality: a specific quantitative gap appears distinct from inspected global realization work. The closest results use the same mathematical mechanisms; the incremental nature and incomplete search coverage are explicitly disclosed.
- The unrestricted Entropy open problems and exploratory general WR/WR0 classifications from v0.1 remain outside the focused result. Do not count these as resolved, and do not advertise them as new theorems.
- Scientific validity: same-species, deterministic mass action and exact edge-wise conservation are model assumptions; no empirical or stochastic equivalence and no assertion of high-order elementary physical reactions.
- Author and journal decisions require human responsibility and confirmation. AI assistance was substantial; no journal submission has been performed.
